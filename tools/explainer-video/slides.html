1371car.s += car.speed * dt; // speed × time
requestAnimationFrame
The browser calls you before each screen refresh.
dt = seconds since last frame
60 Hz → 0.0167 · 120 Hz → 0.0083
Same speed on every screen.
The clamp
Tab away for 10 s → dt = 10 → car teleports 340 m, through a bus.
Math.min caps it at 0.05 s.

Idea 4 / 9 · Physics

Physics is a few numbers, updated every frame

car.air -= HOP_COST; // 16% gas
// Space held, in the air:

air

Idea 5 / 9 · Collisions

Two boxes. Do they overlap?

1531ox = (CAR_HW + o.w/2) - |dx|
oz = (CAR_HL + o.d/2) - |dz|
1533if (ox <= 0 || oz <= 0) continue;

Same idea as two getBoundingClientRect() rects intersecting.

Overlap? then check height:

car.y ≥ roof → cleared, +swag

car.y < roof → crash

impact ≤ 12 → just tailgating

hitCd = 0.6 s, refreshed on contact

Idea 9 / 9 · Debuggability

Put a handle on window

{s: 535.6, x: -3, y: 0, vy: 0, speed: 34, air: 100, onGround: true}

236

Like exposing your Redux store for DevTools.

sim() = fixed 1/60 s steps, no drawing. A bot drives all 3.3 km in a quarter-second.
